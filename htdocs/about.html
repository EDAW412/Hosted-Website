About Us

Welcome to Velnor — launched in 2025, this proudly South African C2C platform is designed to connect local buyers and sellers while empowering our community. Our mission is to create a secure and user-friendly space where South Africans can trade goods, support local entrepreneurs, and thrive in a vibrant digital economy.

By keeping the majority of our business operations and income within South Africa, we aim to stimulate local economic growth and promote homegrown innovation. We are committed to helping South African entrepreneurs succeed in a competitive digital marketplace.

Security is a top priority. Our advanced payment systems, dedicated admin team, and responsive customer support work around the clock to ensure your safety and peace of mind while using our services.
